Walk the way of prayer - Wickhambrook Labyrinth

The Glebe field next to Wickhambrook All Saints' church now has a labyrinth described in it. Its design has been mown out of the grass itself. The labyrinth allows time for prayerful reflection or can be just a bit of fun. Come and experience it and see which fits your needs. Further information can be found in the accompanying Labyrinth Prayer Card that can also be picked up from the church.

Wickhambrook Labyrinth

Come and enjoy the Labyrinth.

The idea and creation was that of the Rector - Brin Singleton.
