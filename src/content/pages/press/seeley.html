Bishop Martin's Deanery visit to All Saints' Wickhambrook

The Bishop of St Edmundsbury and Ipswich, The Rt Revd Martin Seeley will be visiting our Deanery (Deanery of Clare) on the evening of Monday 30th November. There will be a short act of worship in All Saints' church Wickhambrook starting at 7.30pm followed by a "Question and Answer" session. This will be followed by light refreshments and a change to meet and greet with the Bishop.

Bishop Martin Seeley

Everyone is warmly invited to join us at this event and to take the opportunity to meet with the Bishop.
