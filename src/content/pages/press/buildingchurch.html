Building Church

Bishop Nigel [Credit - The Diocese of St Edmundsbury and Ipswich]

Bishop Nigel is looking forward to meeting members of parish congregations at three special summer events. "As we approach our centenary, this will be an opportunity to share the good news of our work present and past, and take stock of the exciting possibilities open to us as we set about our next hundred years," he says.

The afternoons run between 2pm and 6pm. An extensive exhibition staffed by diocesan officers and staff will be open at either end of the afternoon. A centrepiece presentation hosted by Bishop Nigel and others run between 3.30pm and 5pm.

Saturday 13th June - Adastral Park, Martlesham

Saturday 20th June - Rowley Mile Conference Centre, Newmarket

Saturday 27th June - Wedding Marquee, Athelington Hall
