Rector of the Bansfield Benefice

Date for the Institution and Induction

The date for the Institution and Induction of the Revd Eve Bell as Rector of the Bansfield Benefice has been set for Monday 3 February 2020 at 7.30pm at Wickhambrook All Saints parish church. The service will be led by the Bishop and Archdeacon.

Diocesan Announcement

The Right Revd Martin Seeley, the Bishop of St Edmundsbury and Ipswich, is delighted to announce that The Revd Eve Bell has been appointed as part-time Rector of the Benefice of Bansfield. Eve currently has permission to officiate (PTO) in the Thingoe Deanery.

The Bishop has asked me to pass on his thanks to the parish representatives and all those involved in the process of Eve’s appointment, and he assures you of his prayers. Please pray for Eve as she prepares to take up her new post.

Revd Eve Bell
