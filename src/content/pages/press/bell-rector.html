Rector of the Bansfield Benefice

Institution, Induction and Installation

The Institution, Induction and Installation of the Revd Eve Bell as Rector of the Bansfield Benefice was held on Monday 3 February 2020 at 7.30pm at Wickhambrook All Saints parish church. The service was led by Bishop Mike and Archdeacon David. What a lovely evening. A joyous service followed by lovely refreshments and an opportunity to chat with Bishop Mike. Eve is the fourth rector of the Bansfield Benefice since its creation in 1999.

Revd Eve Bell
