Introduction

The existing All Saints' Church, Stansfield is large for a small village and stands towards the top of Plough hill to the north of the village. Its tower is visible, especially when lit up at night, for several miles when approaching the village. The present church which is built of flint rubble faced with freestone is but the latest of many Churches to exist on this site. Every generation has worshipped here and has repaired, improved, restored or rebuilt the church to create our existing church.
