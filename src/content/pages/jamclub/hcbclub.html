Hot Cross Bun Club (HCBC)

During the incumbency of Revd Ian Finn (1999 - 2007) in Holy Week (the week before Easter) up to 50 different children attended the Hot Cross Bun Club annually. This was organised and supervised by Revd Ian Finn and members of the churches of the Bansfield Benefice. Children came from all seven parishes and beyond to take part in activities based around the themes of Holy Week.

These activities included: music, games and refreshments. The themes for the Hot Cross Bun Club are based on the events of Holy Week. To see a little of what has gone on some of the previous years click on the links below in the table.

It is hoped that during the next incumbency that the Hot Cross Bun Club can be restarted during Holy Week.
