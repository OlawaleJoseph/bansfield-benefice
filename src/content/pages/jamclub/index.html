Jesus And Me (JAM) Club

Due to falling numbers of children at Jam Club and job commitments for those running Jam Club, regretfully we have had to take the decision to suspend this valuable children's resource for the moment. However a family service continues on the third Sunday of each month at Wickhambrook church (9.30am). Why not come along and enjoy this simple, easy going service.

September 2009
