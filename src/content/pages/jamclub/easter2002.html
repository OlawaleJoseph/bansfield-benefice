Hot Cross Bun Club 2002

During Holy Week 2002 (the week before Easter) up to 50 different children attended the Hot Cross Bun Club organised and supervised by the Vicar, (Revd Ian Finn) and members of the churches of the Bansfield Benefice. Children came from all seven parishes and beyond to take part in activities based around the themes of Holy Week. As usual the children met in Wickhambrook church hall.
