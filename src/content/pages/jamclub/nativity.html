JAM Club Nativity Play

The JAM Club Nativity Scene created for Christmas 2007

The Jam Club Nativity Play will be held at All Saints' Wickhambrook - 7.00pm (22nd Dec 2011) during their carol service. Following the carols and nativity play there will be refreshments in church. All are welcome so please come along and join in the festivities

May the joy of the angels,
the eagerness of the shepherds,
the perseverance of the wise men,
the obedience of Joseph and Mary,
and the peace of the Christ-child
be yours this Christmas;
and the blessing of God Almighty,
the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit,
be among you and remain with you always.
