Hot Cross Bun Club 2000

During Holy Week 2000 (the week before Easter) up to 50 different children attended the Hot Cross Bun Club organised and supervised by the Vicar, (Revd Ian Finn) and members of the churches of the Bansfield Benefice. Children came from all seven parishes and beyond to take part in activities based around the themes of Holy Week.

The Children made a series of posters depicting the events of Holy Week
Each day there was a different theme and the events leading up to Easter Sunday and the Resurrection were covered.
