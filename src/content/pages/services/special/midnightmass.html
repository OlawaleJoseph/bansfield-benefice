We celebrate midnight mass at one of the Benefice churches, each year. This is always a very popular service so it is advised you get to church a good 15 minutes before the start. The service is a celebration of the birth of Christ in Bethlehem over two thousand years ago. It is a very joyous occasion with many of the most famous and liked Christmas carols being sung. Please try and join us for this great celebration.
