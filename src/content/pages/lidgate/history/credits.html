Credits

The congregation of Lidgate congratulate Mr. Roy Tricker on completing his third Guide in our benefice, and gratefully thank him for his devotion, scholarship and enthusiasm.

The writer is in turn grateful to Becky Carlberg and Betty Whitefield for the illustrations, also to Mr. Birkin Haward for information about the glass and to the Pupils of Copleston High School, Ipswich, for assembling the booklets.

July 1989
