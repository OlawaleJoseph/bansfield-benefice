To help with the fund raising effort and to bring people into this ancient building. All Saints' Church PCC is very honoured and proud to announce that this season the Wickhambrook Players are going to stage their annual play in All Saints' church.

Entitled "Go, Go Gabriel" it is a Brand New Rock Nativity.

Music by Andy Salisbury

Lyrics by Jim Fieldsend

Performances are from Saturday 19th January 2002 till Saturday 26th January 2002 (matinees on Saturdays and Sunday).

Tickets are priced £7.00 (£5.00 Concessions)

Come and join us for the best entertainment anywhere.
