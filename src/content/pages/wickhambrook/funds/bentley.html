Finest Cars of Yesteryear


On Sunday August 21st at Doctor's Barn, Wickhambrook, from noon till 4pm we will be holding our long awaited Bentley to Bugatti BBQ when a fabulous selection of priceless cars will be brought together for all to see. In addition to a selection of over 40 Bentleys, Bugattis and Ferraris there will be attractions for all the family.

These include:

  • Bouncy Castle
  • Face Painting
  • Family BBQ
  • Treasure Hunt
  • Face Painting
  • Stalls
  • Bar
  • Raffle

Something for the car enthusiast and the whole family
