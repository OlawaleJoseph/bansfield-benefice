Wednesday 24th May 2006 from 7pm to 9pm

In conjunction with the Wickhambrook History Society on Wednesday the 24th of May from 7pm to 9pm Clive Paine, Historian, author and broadcaster will talk about All Saints' church giving a unique insight into the building. It promises to be a most stimulating event. Tickets are £5 and include refreshments. For further information and tickets please email contact@bansfieldbenefice.org.uk.
