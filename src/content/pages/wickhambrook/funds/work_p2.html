Phase 2 - Nave North & South Aisle Roofs

Phase 2 - South Aisle Roof

Work started on Phase 2 in February 2003 by R & J Hogg Ltd. This work is confined to the nave aisle roofs. The architect is Tony Redman of the Whitworth Co-partnership.

The work undertaken includes: Re-leading the roof; repairing the parapets; repairing and treating rotted joists and repairing the guttering. The lead roof has to be made as a two phase drop and as such requires the addition of a stainless steel joist to take the weight.
