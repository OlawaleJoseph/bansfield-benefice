Ready to go! (From Feb 2003)

The very good news is that English Heritage has given us a grant of £110,000 on grant-aid-able work of £143,000 to start Phase 2 of the restoration of All Saints' Church. Of that sum we will be able to claim the VAT back which will be in the order of £13,000. Thus we need to raise £20,000 over the next two years.

There is also other none grant-aid-able work that will also have to addressed. This includes the very serious problem we currently have with the heating.
