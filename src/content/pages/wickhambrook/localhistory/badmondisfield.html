Badmondisfield Hall: Elizabethan moated manor house, possibly built by Sir George Somerset who was in possession (circa 1559). It has even older antecedents pre-dating 1066 (former aisled hall house). It is one of the few buildings from this area mentioned in the Domesday Book.
