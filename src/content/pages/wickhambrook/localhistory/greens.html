There are 11 greens in Wickhambrook which places the village in the Guinness Book of Records with the greatest number of village greens in one village. The following are pictures of a few of them.

This is a picture of the United Reformed church in Wickhambrook at Meeting Green off Cemetery Road. The picture is taken from the South.


This picture is of Coltsfoot Green Wickhambrook. This picture is taken from the West. Interestingly the photograph is entitled Colts Crossing Green and this name may have preceded the present name Coltsfoot Green.
