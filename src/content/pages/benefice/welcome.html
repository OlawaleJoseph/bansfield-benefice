Dear visitor,

Thank you for visiting our website, we do hope you will find it interesting and informative. Our Benefice is made up of seven rural Anglican parishes in West Suffolk, in the Diocese of St. Edmundsbury and Ipswich. Each of our seven parishes has a beautiful church building, with its own distinctive character. Each of them has regular services, in addition to being focal points and spiritual beacons for their local communities. Please do take some time to browse the pages of this website to find out more about our lovely parishes and buildings as well as all that we do as Christian communities - our fellowship and our work to spread the love of God in Christ to all.

The Church is led by the Rector assisted by a Lay Elder, who participate actively in the services and work of the church here, along with many other lay people. Although one Benefice, with its shared values and identity, we are also seven quite distinct parishes with our own individual community needs, strengths and weaknesses.

Do come and visit our churches if you would like to, and please join us for worship if you can. You will be made most welcome.
