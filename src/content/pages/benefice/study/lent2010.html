When I survey … Christ's Cross and ours

Course starts Monday 22nd February, and the following four weeks on Monday evenings at 7.30pm.

The Lent Course is entitled "When I survey … Christ's Cross and ours". Each group member needs a course booklet which is available at the first session - cost £3. There is also an accompanying CD in which distinguished Christian speakers give input into each session. All are very welcome.
