Our Lent Course this year for Churches Together and the Bansfield Benefice meets on the Tuesdays of Lent at 2.00pm in the Benefice Hall. Everyone is very welcome.

The Lent Study group meeting follows the Lent Lunches also in the Benefice Hall which starts at 1pm. Again all are welcome to join us.

There will also be the opportunity to chat informally over tea or coffee. Please consider joining us, if you are able to.

Tuesdays at 2.00pm in the Benefice Hall, next to All Saints' Church, Wickhambrook.
