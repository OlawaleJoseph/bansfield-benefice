As part of the mission of the Church and indeed our benefice we aim to donate money to good causes and charities which are local, national and international. There are the obvious ones such as Christian Aid and The Children's Society but also organisations such as Shelter and Samarian's Purse are also supported. We usually target particular times of year such as Christian Aid Week, Christingle, Harvest Festivals, Carol services, Midnight Mass and the Christmas period in general.

Charities we support

  • Bethlehem Arab Society for Rehabilitation
  • Children's Society
  • Christian Aid
  • Christingle Service - Children's Society
  • Harvest Festivals - Local old people - Produce
  • Link Diocese of Kagera in Tanzania
  • Samaritan's Purse
  • Shelter
  • Toy service - Bury St Edmunds children in need - Christmas presents via The Gatehouse
  • Traidcraft
