Churches Together

  • Bansfield Benefice churches
  • Wickhambrook Methodist church
  • Wickhambrook United Reformed church (closed 2021)
  • Kirtling Roman Catholic church

Ecumenical work continues with our local churches together, meeting for worship on our "Walk of Witness" during Holy Week and supporting Christian Aid week, Week of Prayer for Unity, and a monthly Traidcraft coffee morning on the first Saturday of each month in the Benefice Hall. We have as Anglicans, great fellowship with our brothers and sisters of the Methodist church, United Reformed church, Roman Catholic Church (Wickhambrook Methodist and United Reform churches and Kirtling RC church).

The Church of England maintains special relationships with the Methodist Church of Great Britain (through the Anglican-Methodist Covenant signed in 2003) and with the Moravian Church of Britain and Ireland (through the Fetter Lane Agreement signed in 1995.

The Church of England maintains regular bilateral links with the Roman Catholic Church in England and Wales through the English Anglican-Roman Catholic Commission.
