Keyholders

Outside of normal services the church is usually locked. Please see our Heritage visits page for details on how to arrange a visit.

Facilities For Disabled People

The church does have wheelchair access at all times into the church via two ramps. These conform as closely to Health and Safety requirements as can reasonably be expected in a church of its age and design. A public production held in the church of Go, Go Gabriel passed such an inspection in January 2002.

There are no toilet facilities at the church at all, but there is a PA system for amplification for the hearing impaired. Large print service and hymn books are available.

For more information about disability issues in churches visit http://www.throughtheroof.org/.
