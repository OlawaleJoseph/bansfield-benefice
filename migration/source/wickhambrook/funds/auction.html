Black Tie dinner dance with auction (17-05-03)

We are holding a Black Tie dinner and dance with auction at the Memorial Hall on Saturday 17th May (Tickets £25). There will be some really fabulous items for auction, a real "not to be missed" event. The full list is shown below. Items will be available for viewing at the Memorial Hall from 3-4pm on the day of the event. Postal bids for items are possible. Please complete a form found in the Wickhambrook Scene or from the Post Office Shop.

Click on the thumb nail images to see the full sized image.
