The Bumstead Boys (4-11-06)

"Ruby and her horses" - This is a true life narrative about Ruby's love of horses set against the hard life in the agricultural depression of the 1920s and 30s. The performance comprises some accordion playing, a show of digitally enhanced photographs and narrative. Tickets are £7. The performance is for 7-30pm with a prompt start at 8-pm. There will be light refreshments.
