The Old Vicarage

The Old Vicarage

The original vicarage to the church, on the east side and now privately owned, dates back in part to the 14th century. The last incumbent to live in the Vicarage was Revd Cooke. After his departure the vicarage was sold into private ownership by the diocese. Apart from the reproduction of part of "Piers the Ploughman", there are other interesting photographs which have been made available from the current owner of the Old Vicarage.
