Press and News - 15th May 2016

Bishop Mike's Benefice Pentecost visit to Saint Margaret's Cowlinge

The Suffragan Bishop of Dunwich, The Rt Revd Dr Mike Harrison will be visiting our benefice to help us celebrate Pentecost at St Margaret's church Cowlinge on Sunday 15th May 2016 at 11.00am. After the service there will be light refreshments and a change to meet and greet with the Bishop.

Bishop Mike Harrison

Everyone is warmly invited to join us at this event and to take the opportunity to meet with the Bishop.
